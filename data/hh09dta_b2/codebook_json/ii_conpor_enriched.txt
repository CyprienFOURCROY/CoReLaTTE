Column "anio" means: Year of visit.

Column "anioprox" means: Year of next visit.

Column "folio" means: Household ID.

Column "hrs" means: Time Interview started: hrs.

Column "hrsfin" means: Time Interview ended: hrs.

Column "hrsprox" means: Time next visit: hrs.

Column "mes" means: Month visit.

Column "mesprox" means: Month next visit.

Column "min" means: Time Interview: min.

Column "minfin" means: Time Interview ended: min.

Column "minprox" means: Time next visit: min.

Column "rev" means: Result of visit.

Column "secuencia" means: Sequence Day.

Column "visita" means: Number of visits.
